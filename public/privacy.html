Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Oscal helps people support creators by watching ad videos, sending wishes, and appearing in a creator's activity feed. This policy explains what information Oscal collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Creator account details from YouTube sign-in, such as display name, channel identifier, and profile image.
  • Support activity, including creator page, support count, wish or reaction, badge state, timestamps, and related content title or platform.
  • Anonymous supporter identifiers used to count supports, unlock badges, and remember supporter activity.
  • Saved supporter profile details if a supporter chooses to add them, such as display name and profile picture.
  • Device and browser signals used for daily limits, abuse prevention, and blocking creator self-support.
  • Basic server request data that hosting providers normally process, such as IP address, browser user agent, request time, and requested URL.

How We Use Information

  • To run support pages, dashboards, ad watches, wishes, badges, and activity feeds.
  • To prevent abuse, repeated support spam, bot behavior, and creator self-support.
  • To keep support counts and creator stats synced across browsers and devices.
  • To debug errors, protect the service, and improve the product.

What Other People Can See

Creator pages may show support activity in the deep end, including an anonymous supporter label or a saved supporter name, profile image, badge, wish, reaction, and time. Creator dashboards show creator support stats and recent support activity.

TikTok Ad Links

When an ad video is clicked while it is playing, Oscal may open a TikTok ad link in a new tab. TikTok is a third-party service and may collect information under its own privacy policy. Oscal does not control TikTok's tracking, cookies, or account behavior.

Storage and Retention

Oscal stores app data so support counts, badges, wishes, and profiles can continue working across restarts and deployments. Data may be kept while the app is operating, unless it is deleted during maintenance, reset for a demo, or removed after a privacy request.

Sharing

Oscal does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers needed to run the app, and by third-party sites users choose to open, such as TikTok.

Your Choices

  • You can use Oscal as an anonymous supporter without saving a profile.
  • You can avoid clicking ad videos if you do not want to open third-party ad links.
  • You can request help with removing saved supporter or creator data through the channel where you received access to Oscal.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Oscal operator through the same account, creator, or channel that gave you access to the app.
